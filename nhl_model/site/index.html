How this works

Model: team offense (goals + xG), team xG against and goalie GSAx factor, all walk-forward with empty-net goals removed, then blended with Pinnacle's no-vig line. A game is a BET only when the edge at the best available price is above the threshold and both starting goalies are confirmed. Stake is fractional Kelly. CLV compares the flagged price with Pinnacle's no-vig closing line, which is the fastest read on whether the edge is real.
